# gta-6-spoiler-blocker

GTA 6 is the biggest game to release, and the spoilers are going to flood the internet when it drops.

The solution?

This app!

HOW IT WORKS:

The app checks the text on your screen for GTA 6 spoiler keywords. When it finds one, it covers that spot with a blur or blackout.

FEATURES
• 4 keyword levels: Simple, Moderate and Extreme, Custom
• Add your own keywords
• Choose which apps are monitored
• Blur or blackout cover style
• URL whitelist, so specific pages you trust don't get blocked
• Optional "Whole video" mode for short-video feeds: covers the full video and mutes media sound until you scroll past it
• Notification blocking
And some additional features/settings

PRIVACY
Everything is checked on your device. Screen text is not stored, shared or sent anywhere. The app collects no data. Only your settings are saved on your phone.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE DISCLOSURE
This app uses Android's Accessibility Service to read the text and content descriptions on your screen in the apps you choose to monitor, and to place the cover where the spoiler is. It does not read what you type or password fields. The service only runs if you turn it on in Android settings, and you can turn it off at any time. Optional notification blocking uses Android's Notification access, which also needs its own switch.
